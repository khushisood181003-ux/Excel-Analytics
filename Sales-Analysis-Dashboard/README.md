# Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents a **Sales Analysis Dashboard** developed in Microsoft Excel to analyze sales, expenses, profit, product performance, regional performance, and quarterly trends.

The dashboard transforms a small sales dataset into a concise business analysis using **Excel formulas, PivotTables, Pivot Charts, and interactive slicers**.

---

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to evaluate overall sales performance and identify the products, regions, and quarters contributing most to sales and profitability.

The analysis focuses on:

* Tracking total sales, expenses, and profit
* Measuring overall profit margin
* Comparing performance across regions
* Identifying the most profitable product
* Analyzing quarterly sales and profit
* Generating actionable business recommendations

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains **16 sales records** with the following variables:

| Column     | Description         |
| ---------- | ------------------- |
| `Product`  | Product category    |
| `Sales`    | Sales generated     |
| `Expenses` | Expenses incurred   |
| `Profit`   | Profit generated    |
| `Region`   | Sales region        |
| `Quarter`  | Quarter of the year |

The dataset contains three products (**Product A, Product B, and Product C**), two regions (**Region 1 and Region 2**), and four quarters (**Q1–Q4**).

## Key Analytical Insights

### 1. Quarterly Performance

**Q4 is the strongest quarter**, generating the highest sales of **₹1,240** and the highest total profit of **₹660**.

### 2. Regional Performance

**Region 2 slightly outperforms Region 1**, generating total sales of **₹1,980** compared with **₹1,880** for Region 1.

### 3. Product Performance

**Product A is the most profitable product**, generating a total profit of **₹1,000** across the analyzed records.

### 4. Overall Profitability

The business generated **₹3,860 in total sales and ₹2,080 in total profit**, resulting in an overall **profit margin of 53.89%**.

---

## Business Recommendation

Based on the analysis:

> **Focus on Product A in Region 2, as it currently delivers the highest overall performance.**

The combination of the strongest-performing product and region represents a potential area for maintaining or increasing business focus.
